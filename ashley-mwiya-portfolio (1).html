About

the real story

So here’s how I got here — I started making Instagram stories for a friend’s shop, and I got obsessed with figuring out why one post worked and another one just... didn’t. That obsession turned into Google Ads certs, then AI-powered marketing training, and now I genuinely can’t run a campaign without also making sure the landing page and the creative are solid.

Most of my work is with real estate agents and restaurants — people who need to turn attention into action. A property listing that needs more eyeballs, a restaurant trying to pack out a Friday night. I love this space because the wins are real: a viewing gets booked, someone messages "is this still available?", a table fills up. I handle the ad side and the design side so nothing gets lost in the handoff.
